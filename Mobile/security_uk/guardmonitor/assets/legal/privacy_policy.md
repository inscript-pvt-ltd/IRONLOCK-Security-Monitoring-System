# IronLock Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 July 2026
Last Updated: 24 July 2026

## 1. Introduction

IronLock is a security workforce management platform designed to help authorised security companies manage personnel, monitor active shifts, improve operational safety, and maintain accurate security records.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the IronLock mobile application, how we use it, how we protect it, and the rights available to users.

IronLock is intended for use by authorised security organisations operating in the United Kingdom. We process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

By using the IronLock application, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Data Controller and Data Processor

The organisation that deploys and uses IronLock (your employer or security company) acts as the Data Controller and determines why and how personal data is processed.

IronLock acts as the Data Processor, processing personal data only on behalf of and according to the instructions of the Data Controller.

## 3. Information We Collect

Depending on your role and authorised access, IronLock may collect the following categories of information.

### Personal Information

- Full name
- Employee or staff identification number
- Username
- Email address
- Telephone number
- Security licence information (including SIA licence details where applicable)

This information is used to create and manage authorised user accounts.

### Location Information

IronLock accesses your device location only while you are actively working and signed into an authorised shift.

Location information is used to:

- Verify attendance
- Confirm presence within assigned work locations
- Support operational monitoring
- Assist emergency response when required

IronLock does not monitor your location outside working hours. The application stores only the user's current active location and does not maintain a continuous location history or travel route.

### Camera Access

IronLock uses your device camera to capture live verification photographs during authorised work shifts.

Verification photographs are used only to:

- Confirm attendance
- Verify presence at assigned locations
- Support security compliance requirements

IronLock does not use facial recognition technology or collect biometric data.

### Authentication and Security Information

To protect user accounts and maintain system security, IronLock records:

- Login timestamps
- Authentication activity
- Device information
- Session information
- Security logs

### Incident and Operational Records

Users may submit operational information including:

- Incident reports
- Welfare check responses
- Shift attendance records
- Verification events
- Security-related activities

These records are maintained to support security operations and compliance requirements.

### Push Notifications

IronLock uses secure push notification services to deliver:

- Welfare check reminders
- Emergency alerts
- Shift notifications
- Important operational updates

Push notification services do not receive the contents of your personal records.

## 4. Information We Do Not Collect

IronLock does not:

- Record audio or phone conversations
- Access contacts
- Read text messages
- Collect biometric information
- Track users outside authorised working hours
- Store continuous location history or travel routes
- Sell personal information

## 5. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through IronLock to:

- Authenticate authorised users
- Manage user accounts
- Verify attendance and shift participation
- Confirm presence at assigned locations
- Conduct welfare checks
- Record operational incidents
- Improve employee safety
- Maintain security and audit records
- Protect the integrity and security of the platform
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We process personal information only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable law.

## 6. Legal Basis for Processing

Where UK GDPR applies, personal information is processed on one or more of the following legal bases:

- Legitimate interests
- Performance of an employment or service contract
- Compliance with legal obligations
- Protection of vital interests where necessary

## 7. Data Sharing

Personal information may be shared only with:

- Your employer or authorised security organisation
- Authorised administrators responsible for managing security operations
- Trusted service providers who support the operation of IronLock (such as secure cloud hosting and push notification services)

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

## 8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information, including:

- HTTPS encrypted communications
- Encrypted data storage
- Strong password hashing
- Role-based access controls
- Secure authentication
- Access logging and monitoring
- Secure cloud infrastructure hosted within the United Kingdom where applicable

Although we use industry-standard security measures, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to:

- Provide the IronLock service
- Support operational requirements
- Meet contractual obligations
- Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

Retention periods are determined by the Data Controller.

## 10. Your Rights

Where applicable, you may have the right to:

- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of personal information
- Restrict processing
- Object to certain processing activities
- Request a copy of your personal information in a portable format

Some information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations. Requests should be submitted to your employer or system administrator.

## 11. Children's Privacy

IronLock is intended solely for professional use by authorised personnel and is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 12. International Data Transfers

IronLock is designed for organisations operating within the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred outside the United Kingdom, appropriate safeguards will be implemented in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal obligations. When material changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be revised.

## 14. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of your personal information, please contact your employer or your authorised IronLock system administrator.

If required by law, you may also contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the United Kingdom's independent authority for data protection.

IronLock is committed to protecting your privacy while providing a secure, reliable, and transparent security workforce management platform.
